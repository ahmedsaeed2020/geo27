## enterprise component rules
- Brand bands carry up to three live chips: a page passes `chips` to ContextStrip, otherwise the shared Dashboard Designer publishes its first three KPI cards through enterprise/kit/heroFeed.ts (same figure and drill, first band on the page only); the DIGIT mark shows only on a band without figures, so no app draws its own hero.
- Chart rules (at most six categorical colours then «أخرى», insight + range footer ChartFoot, waterfall as rows when its panel is narrow) live in dashboards/views.tsx and enterprise/kit/charts.tsx; /dev/visual-audit (lib/visualAudit.ts, Developer mode) scans pages for the visual rules with zero findings as the gate.
- Approval queue pieces (enterprise/kit/ApprovalQueue.tsx state pills, «بانتظار اعتمادك», decision menu, approval drawer, bulk bar), queue split + change log (kit/QueueSplit.tsx over lib/changeLog.ts), stage columns (kit/StageBoard.tsx), budget bar (kit/BudgetBar.tsx) and save/share view (enterprise/SaveShareView.tsx over lib/sharedViews.ts) are app-agnostic: apps pass rows, states and actions in, so every app reuses one look and behaviour.
- Document Hub registers with approval columns render compact (`data-hub-compact`: tighter cells, long state pills wrap, age under the date, 360 under the number from sm up) and a row amount falls back to the DocType's own list-view Currency field (hubRowAmount), so every column incl. the amount fits at 1440 and no row shows "—" under a totalled register.
- Cost display pieces (enterprise/kit/CostKit.tsx: UseLine, RemainingBar, BudgetSheet, RemainingRing, EquationBand, PeriodStrips) take figures, levels and links from the app and compute nothing, so any app's cost screen shares one look.
- Finance home pieces (enterprise/kit/FinanceKit.tsx: Signed, ViewToggle, AccountCompare, ShortcutChips) and the «أضف عنصرًا +» slot (kit/AddSlot.tsx, provided by DashboardDesigner `addSlot`, claimed by the first KpiRow on the page, adds into the personal layout) take figures, drills and widget lists from the app and compute nothing, so every app shares one finance look and one add path.
- Reconciliation pieces (enterprise/kit/ReconKit.tsx SelectionBar / PaymentScore / UpcomingList + dueTag; enterprise/BankRecon.tsx ReconEquations / ReconStrips / CloseStrip / NeedMatch) take figures from lib/coreShared/bankRecon.ts or the app and compute nothing, so Pro and Enterprise finance pages share one reconciliation look.
- Dispatch pieces (enterprise/kit/DispatchKit.tsx: DayBoard hour lanes with drop, conflicts, travel gaps and actual bars; RouteMap numbered pins on map tiles) take rows, blocks and pins from the app and compute nothing; the week view is the shared WeekBoard, so every app with crews shares one board look.
- Inspection pieces (enterprise/kit/InspectKit.tsx: LocationTree rings, OutcomePad with reason-required outcomes, BigRing, AlertBanner, ReportSheet + SheetSection + Bil two-language labels) take figures and callbacks from the app and compute nothing, so every printed or walked document shares one look.
- Field pieces (enterprise/kit/FieldKit.tsx + field.css: DayStops, StepBar, ThumbBar, OutcomePad, SignaturePad, RequirementCards, FlowBar, HoursBar, StockCard, SyncChip, OfflineBanner) take rows, states and handlers from the app and compute nothing, so every field app shares one phone journey look.
- Page tables join the Enterprise look through enterprise/kit/EntTable.tsx (head + rows, or `wrap` around the page's own table): strip from the row state attribute or state pills, header filters, search/count and saved views (List Filter `table:<id>`) are read from the rendered rows only, so no page logic or data changes and phones get card rows.
